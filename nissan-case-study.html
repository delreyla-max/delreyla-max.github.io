Creative DirectionBrand SystemLaunch Ecosystem

Created a North American brand system that gave Nissan a consistent visual language while allowing each vehicle launch to keep its own personality.

PROBLEM

Nissan needed a cohesive North American brand system that could create consistency across vehicles, campaigns, retail environments, and digital touchpoints.

SOLUTION

Helped develop a flexible visual framework for Nissan's North American marketing ecosystem, extending brand strategy into vehicle-specific color worlds, campaign layouts, environmental graphics, digital experiences, and launch materials.

IMPACT

The system created a scalable foundation for multiple Nissan launch campaigns, including LEAF, Quest, Versa, and Juke, while keeping each vehicle distinct and recognizably Nissan.
